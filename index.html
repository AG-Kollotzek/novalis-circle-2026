Towards Standardized Benchmarking of SGRT Technologies

An open motion phantom and evaluation chain for quantifying the tracking accuracy of ExacTrac Dynamic Surface.

Buck T.1, Bersenkowitsch N.2, Bodner L.2, Ganswindt U.1, Kollotzek S.1

  1. 1 Department of Radiation Oncology, Medical University of Innsbruck, Austria
  2. 2 Department of Radiation Oncology, Tirol Kliniken, Austria
  • Poster Development and Validation of a Mechatronic Phantom for Quantitative Tracking-Performance QA A0 PDF · 2.4 MB
  • Talk From Commissioning to Long-Term Quality Assurance slides · added after the talk

ESTRO-ACROP specifies a tracking-performance tolerance of 1 mm and 1° for surface-guided radiotherapy but no measurement method. A motorised three-axis phantom with two heated surface pads, the SURF Test Unit, serves as ground truth for ExacTrac Dynamic Surface (ETD). Phantom and ETD logs are synchronised, transformed to six degrees of freedom and compared per sample; the same chain is used for method validation and for the yearly QA of each linac.

Code & data

  • surf-etds-phantom

    The SURF Test Unit: firmware, measurement terminal, hardware documentation and ground-truth motion data.

  • surf-etds-data

    Raw logs of the phantom and of ExacTrac Dynamic Surface, recorded at the linear accelerators of Tirol Kliniken.

  • surf-etds-analysis

    Evaluation pipeline: synchronisation, calibrated kinematics with uncertainty propagation, metrics, statistics and figures.

  • surf-etds-qa

    Yearly QA: pass-rate classification, calibration against X-ray sphere detection and one report per linac.
