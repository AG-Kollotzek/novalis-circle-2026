Towards Standardized Benchmarking of SGRT Technologies:
Development and Validation of the Mechatronic Phantom Behind Quantitative Tracking-Performance QA

Buck T.1, Bersenkowitsch N.2, Bodner L.2, Entfellner P.2, Ganswindt U.1, Kollotzek S.1
1 Department of Radiation Oncology, Medical University of Innsbruck, Austria
2 Department of Radiation Oncology, Tirol Kliniken, Austria

An in-house mechatronic phantom that drives known, reproducible motion so the tracking performance of a surface-guided radiotherapy system can be measured rather than estimated — and the full path from raw motor logs to a signed annual QA report.

Table 4 of the ESTRO-ACROP guideline lists D2 — Tracking performance as a required test at 1 mm / 1°, monthly to annually on a C-arm linac. It defines what must be tested, how often and to what tolerance — but not how the number is produced. That gap is the origin of this project.
